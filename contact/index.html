About DatAI.

DatAI — Data for Artificial Intelligence is a research laboratory of the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication at the University of Milano-Bicocca. It was previously known as INSID&S Lab.

The lab develops models and tools at the intersection of data management and artificial intelligence, with public work spanning semantic data, document intelligence, reliable agentic workflows and distributed AI systems.
