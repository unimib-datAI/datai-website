About DatAI.

DatAI — Data for Artificial Intelligence is a research laboratory of the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication at the University of Milano-Bicocca. It was previously known as INSID&S Lab.

The group works across four laboratory rooms in Building U14—1031, 1032, 1033 and T35—and develops models and tools at the intersection of data management and artificial intelligence.

Public work spans semantic data, document intelligence, reliable agentic workflows and distributed AI systems.
