Research profile.

Matteo Palmonari is an Associate Professor at the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication of the University of Milano-Bicocca and a member of the DatAI Lab. His research lies at the intersection of data management and artificial intelligence, with a focus on semantic data integration and enrichment, knowledge graphs, knowledge-intensive NLP and large language models, and neuro-symbolic AI. His recent work investigates the integration of structured knowledge and large language models, with applications in knowledge-intensive domains such as the legal domain.

He has led the University of Milano-Bicocca team in several European and national research and innovation projects, including DataPACT, enRichMyData, euBusinessGraph, EW-Shopp, the PRIN project Discount Quality for Responsible Data Science, and Next Generation UPP. He was the coordinator of the H2020 project EW-Shopp and scientific manager of the Horizon Europe project enRichMyData.
