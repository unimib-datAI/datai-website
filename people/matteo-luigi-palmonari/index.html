Research profile.

Associate Professor at DISCo and UniMiB responsible for DataPACT and enRichMyData. His public work focuses on knowledge graphs, semantic data enrichment, entity linking, knowledge-intensive document analysis and data quality. Institutional and CORDIS sources also document his coordination of EW-Shopp and the UniMiB contribution to euBusinessGraph.
