Data for Artificial Intelligence · Milano

Make data work for AI.

DatAI studies how data management can make AI systems more grounded, inspectable and useful—from semantic enrichment and document intelligence to agentic and distributed systems.

Explore research ↘ Meet the group ↓

D/01Data semantics

What must data mean before AI can use it?

Annotations, knowledge graphs and data enrichment with traceable inputs.

D/02Documents

How do we ask better questions of complex documents?

Structured query, retrieval and evidence-aware generation.

D/03AI systems

How should AI systems behave under real constraints?

Cost, performance and quality across agentic and distributed execution.
